## Outcome

Researchers could publish rich material without breaking it into disconnected parts. Readers moved naturally between notes, transcripts, and media while keeping enough surrounding context to understand why the material mattered.

## What carried forward

This project reinforced how much trust a publishing platform can create through consistency alone. When the layout rules remain stable, readers spend more time with the material and less time rebuilding their bearings.
