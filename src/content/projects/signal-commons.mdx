## Outcome

The index gave the research team a clearer publishing cadence and made the work easier to discover for external readers. Instead of isolated updates, the site behaved more like a coherent body of work.

## What carried forward

This project reinforced the value of content systems that remain easy to author in. The more publishing resembles writing rather than formatting, the more likely a team is to keep using it well.
