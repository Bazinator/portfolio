## Outcome

The final product felt less like software trying to organize everything at once and more like a workspace with a clear reading order. Teams reported that planning reviews became shorter because the interface carried more of the narrative for them.

## What carried forward

The most durable part of the project was not a single component. It was the decision to treat every new surface as part of the same editorial system. That constraint made follow-on work faster because the product no longer needed to invent a new visual language for each feature.
